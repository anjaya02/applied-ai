# Week 1 Notes — AI History: Important MCQ Points

## 1. 1950: Alan Turing → “Computing Machinery and Intelligence”
This is Turing's famous work asking whether machines can think.

**Remember:** `Turing → 1950 → Computing Machinery and Intelligence`

## 2. 1955: John McCarthy → coined the term “Artificial Intelligence”
Meaning he introduced the actual term **Artificial Intelligence**.

**Remember:** `Who coined the term AI? → John McCarthy`

## 3. 1956: Dartmouth meeting → “Artificial Intelligence” adopted
Do not confuse this with 1955.

- `1955 = McCarthy coined the term`
- `1956 = Dartmouth meeting adopted the term`

## 4. Logic Theorist → Newell & Simon → early logical-reasoning AI program
This is especially important because the lecture included an MCQ asking which early program demonstrated logical reasoning.

**Answer:** `Logic Theorist`

## 5. 1980–88 Expert systems BOOM → 1988–93 BUST = AI Winter
AI became commercially popular through expert systems, then that industry collapsed.

That decline is called an **AI Winter**.

**Remember:** `AI Winter → 1988–93 → expert systems bust`

## 6. 2012 onwards → Big Data + Big Compute + Neural Networks
This is the modern AI boom.

More data and much stronger computing helped neural-network approaches become dramatically more successful.
